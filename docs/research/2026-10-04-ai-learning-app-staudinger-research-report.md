# Build the Tutor That Teachers Assign

Research report, as of 4 October 2026. Scope: a web-based, AI-assisted learning app for the German school system, starting at the Staudinger-Gesamtschule in Freiburg (Baden-Württemberg) for grades 7, 10 and 11, running on phones, Apple tablets and PCs, built initially with Anthropic tooling but operated predominantly on local or EU-hosted language models, and designed to become a sellable product. German terms are kept in German with a short gloss on first use. Where the underlying research notes record a gap or a conflict between sources, the report says so rather than smoothing it over. All figures, prices, dates and legal statements carry their source inline.

## Executive summary

**Recommendation in one sentence: build a teacher-assigned practice-and-feedback tutor that is mapped to the Bildungsplan 2016 (the Baden-Württemberg curriculum) at the three Niveaus G, M and E, launch it at the Staudinger-Gesamtschule in grade 10 Mathematik ahead of the Realschulabschlussprüfung (the central school-leaving exam at the end of Klasse 10) on 29 April 2027, extend the same engine to grade 7 and grade 11 Mathematik before summer 2027, add Englisch and Deutsch in 2027/28, run it as an installable web app on EU-hosted open-weight models with Claude reserved for hard turns and for the content pipeline, and sell it to schools and Schulträger (the municipal bodies that fund school equipment) as a flat per-school licence, not to students as another chatbot.**

**What to build first and why maths.** Mathematik is the one subject where the Bildungsplan itemises every Teilkompetenz on the three Niveaus, where the exam has a calculator-free Pflichtteil of 45 minutes, a Wahlteil of two out of three tasks and 50 Punkte in total, all published in the Ausführungsbestimmungen ([KM, RSAP 2027](https://km.baden-wuerttemberg.de/fileadmin/redaktion/m-km/intern/PDF/Dateien/Realschule/Ausführungsbestimmungen_2027/Ausführungsbestimmungen_Realschulabschlussprüfung_2027.pdf)), where every answer can be checked by a computer algebra system rather than trusted to a language model, and where the evidence base is strongest: step-based tutoring systems reach about 0.76 SD in VanLehn's synthesis ([VanLehn 2011](https://www.emergingedtech.com/-kvanlehn/Stringent/PDF/EffectivenessOfTutoring_Vanlehn.pdf)), while K-12-specific syntheses and large product trials land at 0.1 to 0.27 SD ([Leite et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.04997)). The school's Jahrgangsteams already teach all classes of a year group "the same content at almost the same time" with Arbeitspläne (individual work plans) and a Logbuch ([Staudinger, Jahrgangshäuser](https://staudinger-gesamtschule.de/eine-besondere-schule/schulkonzept/jahrgangshaeuser/)), and the school is piloting one hour per day of individualised learning in grades 6 and 7 from 2026/27 ([Staudinger, Individuelles Lernen](https://staudinger-gesamtschule.de/eine-besondere-schule/schulkonzept/individuelles-lernen/)). A tutor that fills those Arbeitsplan hours with G/M/E-tiered practice is the product the school's own routines are asking for. Grade 10 comes first because the exam date creates urgency and a measurable target; grade 7 (Standardstufe 7/8/9 items) and grade 11 (the 24-item Differentialrechnung entry list of the Einführungsphase, [Bildungsplan GMSO Mathematik Klasse 11](https://www.bildungsplaene-bw.de/,Lde/BP2016BW_ALLG_GMSO_M_IK_11_04)) reuse the same engine with different item banks.

**Pedagogical design.** Not a chat window. The two secondary-school results that matter most say the same thing from opposite directions: unguarded chatbot access raised practice scores 48 percent and cut unassisted exam scores 17 percent in a Turkish high-school RCT, while a guarded tutor fed with teacher-supplied solutions removed the harm ([Bastani et al., PNAS 2025](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2422633122)); and in the only two-year school RCT of a commercial AI tutor, students messaged Khanmigo in just 17 percent of the sessions in which they made a mistake, so the small gains (0.04 to 0.05 SD per year) came from structured practice, not from chat ([Oreopoulos and Low 2026](https://edworkingpapers.com/sites/default/files/ai26-1551.pdf)). The design therefore is: teacher assigns from the Arbeitsplan; the student gets a worked example, then faded completion problems, then independent items; step-level hints activate automatically on error; the model always receives the verified solution and the Jahrgangsteam's list of common mistakes; final answers are withheld by default; retrieval, spacing and interleaving are scheduled into the loop; the product KPI is unassisted, delayed performance, not in-app correctness. Teachers get a dashboard of formative summaries that feed the twice-yearly Coachinggespräche ([Staudinger, Coachinggespräche](https://staudinger-gesamtschule.de/eine-besondere-schule/schulkonzept/coachinggespraeche/)) and never a grade, because Baden-Württemberg's rules say AI "dürfen keine Noten vergeben oder andere wesentlichen schulischen Entscheidungen treffen" ([KM, KI im Unterricht](https://km.baden-wuerttemberg.de/de/schule/digitalisierung/kuenstliche-intelligenz-im-unterricht)) and the EU AI Act makes AI that evaluates learning outcomes high-risk ([AI Act Annex III](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/annex/3/)).

**Stack and model mix.** A progressive web app installed as a Home Screen web app: since Safari 26 (15 September 2025) any site added to the Home Screen opens as a standalone app with "zero requirements for installability" ([WebKit, Safari 26.0](https://webkit.org/blog/17333/webkit-features-in-safari-26-0/)), which covers iPhones, school iPads (deployable as an MDM Full Screen Web Clip, [Apple, Web Clips payload](https://support.apple.com/guide/deployment/web-clips-payload-settings-depbc7c7808/web)) and Windows PC pools via Edge. Identity through LTI 1.3 from the school's Moodle BW instance and VIDIS, the Länder's identity broker, which transmits only a pseudonym and accepts web apps only ([VIDIS, Teilnahmeprozess](https://www.vidis.schule/teilnahmeprozess/)). The LLM layer sits behind an OpenAI-compatible gateway: default tutoring turns go to EU-hosted open weights (gpt-oss-120b, Qwen3.8-27B or Gemma 4 on STACKIT, IONOS, OVHcloud or Scaleway, with not every model at every provider) at an estimated EUR 0.01 to 0.09 per student-hour; hard turns (multi-step proofs, essay feedback) and the whole content pipeline (item generation, Bildungsplan tagging, evaluation) go to Claude Sonnet 5.5 at $2 per million input tokens ([Claude pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing)) via Amazon Bedrock EU or Vertex AI europe-west1 at a 10 percent premium, because Anthropic's first-party API offers only "us" and "global" inference ([Claude docs, data residency](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/data-residency)). Claude Code and the Agent SDK are the build tools; vLLM speaks the Anthropic Messages API so prompts port to local models ([vLLM docs](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/latest/)). Every maths claim is verified in SymPy; minors are protected by Llama Guard 4 plus a Haiku screening pass; traces go to Langfuse's EU cloud or a self-hosted instance. A school GPU server is a sovereignty option, not a cost saver: an RTX Pro 6000 workstation (about EUR 12,000 to 14,000, estimate) never breaks even against EU open-weight APIs at EUR 0.01 to 0.03 per student-hour.

**Business.** Free, teacher-first pilot at the Staudinger in 2026/27 under an Auftragsverarbeitungsvertrag (AVV, the GDPR processor contract) with the school as controller on the § 115b SchG basis, a ready-made Datenschutz-Folgenabschätzung (DSFA) and a parent letter, mirroring the state's own fAIrChat pilot pattern. Then a flat school licence of about EUR 2,000 to 5,000 per year (EUR 2 to 5 per student for a 1,000-student school), the shape Sparx and Third Space Learning use and the corridor Khanmigo has fallen to ($15 per student per year, [Khan Academy Districts](https://blog.khanacademy.org/becoming-a-khan-academy-districts-partner/)), paid by the Schulträger, ideally from DigitalPakt 2.0 lump sums (EUR 5 billion to 2030, in force 1 September 2026, [BMBFSFJ](https://www.bmbfsfj.bund.de/bmbfsfj/aktuelles/alle-meldungen/karin-prien-unterzeichnet-verwaltungsvereinbarung-zum-digitalpakt-2-0-293648)), although eligibility of AI software under Baden-Württemberg's not yet published Förderrichtlinie is unconfirmed. A Land framework contract (the fobizz pattern in Rheinland-Pfalz at about EUR 1.3 to 1.4 million per year for a whole Land; bettermarks got a little over EUR 2 million in total to 2028) is the 2028 target after multi-school evidence. Differentiation against the free state chatbot AIS.chat is curriculum mapping, persistent learning records and the teacher loop, none of which AIS.chat offers. In 2026 the only honest claims are teacher time, structured practice and formative feedback; test-score gains must wait for a pre-registered evaluation.

**Biggest moving targets.** The Abitur rules for today's grade 11 (no Leitfaden for Abitur 2029 was online on 4 October 2026); the Commission's draft high-risk guidelines under the AI Act and the profiling trigger that could pull an "adaptive" tutor into Annex III; Baden-Württemberg's DigitalPakt 2.0 Förderrichtlinie; and whether the Staudinger, as a Schule besonderer Art, follows the Gemeinschaftsschule Stundentafel at all, which must be confirmed with the school.

## The school and its curricula: grades 7, 10 and 11

### The Staudinger-Gesamtschule in brief

The Staudinger-Gesamtschule describes itself as an "Integrierte und differenzierte Gesamtschule" and a "Schule der besonderen Art" covering grades 5 to 13 with three Abschluss paths: Hauptschulabschluss after Klasse 9, Realschulabschluss after Klasse 10 and the Abitur after a three-year Oberstufe (Klasse 11, K1 and K2) ([Staudinger, Schule der besonderen Art](https://staudinger-gesamtschule.de/eine-besondere-schule/schulkonzept/schule-der-besonderen-art/)). It is therefore not a Gemeinschaftsschule but one of only three public "Schulen besonderer Art" under § 107 Schulgesetz; the Statistisches Landesamt counts exactly three such schools with 4,187 students and an average class size of 25.8 in 2024/25 ([Statistisches Landesamt BW](https://www.statistik-bw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Service/Veroeff/Statistische_Berichte/323124001.pdf)). Which school is the third is unresolved in the notes: Wikipedia names Heidelberg, other sources name Tübingen ([Wikipedia, IGMH](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/IGMH)); this is irrelevant to the product but worth knowing before quoting "one of three".

The school is large and newly built. Freiburg's Amtsblatt reports 1,192 students, seven parallel classes per year group up to grade 8 and a 110-million-euro Neubau completed in two phases (September 2022 and the start of 2025/26), with Natalie Gros as the new principal ([Amtsblatt Freiburg](https://www.freiburg.de/pb/site/Freiburg/get/documents_E-478749396/freiburg/daten/news/amtsblatt/pdf/Amtsblatt_898_BF.pdf)); the architects designed it for about 1,600 students and 200 teachers in five Jahrgangshaus modules ([Sacker Architekten](https://sacker.de/projekt/staudinger-gesamtschule-freiburg/)), and it was officially inaugurated on 6 November 2025 ([Schwarzwälder Bote](https://www.schwarzwaelder-bote.de/inhalt.neubau-offiziell-eingeweiht-staudinger-gesamtschule-in-freiburg-ist-fuer-die-zukunft-geruestet.05aea4a1-babf-4432-97d9-3b0260eb8ae1.html)). The city brochure for 2026/27 lists the school at Staudingerstraße 10 with differentiated instruction through Klasse 8 and separated classes in Klassen 9 and 10 ([Stadt Freiburg, Weiterführende Schulen 2026/2027](https://www.freiburg.de/pb/site/Freiburg/get/params_E-962088197/1636580/Weiterf_Schulen_2627.pdf)).

The pedagogical concept is the product's natural host. Each Jahrgangshaus is "a small unit within the school"; each class has two fixed Klassenlehrkräfte; all classes of a year group work on the same content at almost the same time with Arbeitspläne, Projektwochen and Klassenarbeiten created by the Jahrgangsteam; from grade 9 there are school-type classes (Gymnasium, Realschule, Werkrealschule, Hauptschule) ([Staudinger, Jahrgangshäuser](https://staudinger-gesamtschule.de/eine-besondere-schule/schulkonzept/jahrgangshaeuser/)). Students work with "niveaudifferenzierte Aufgaben" and reflect in a Logbuch, lessons are usually staffed with two teachers, and from 2026/27 grades 6 and 7 get "eine Stunde pro Tag" of individualised learning while a working group develops guidelines for a consistent lesson structure ([Staudinger, Individuelles Lernen](https://staudinger-gesamtschule.de/eine-besondere-schule/schulkonzept/individuelles-lernen/)). Twice a year, Klassenlehrkräfte hold 20 to 30-minute solution-focused Coachinggespräche in which students name successes, pick an Entwicklungsfeld and agree Lösungsansätze; the protocol feeds 20-minute Eltern-Lehrer-Schüler-Gespräche ([Staudinger, Coachinggespräche](https://staudinger-gesamtschule.de/eine-besondere-schule/schulkonzept/coachinggespraeche/)). It is a gebundene Ganztagesschule, Monday to Friday 8:00 to 16:00 and Wednesday to 13:05, and an Eliteschule des Sports and des Fußballs ([Staudinger, Leitbild](https://staudinger-gesamtschule.de/eine-besondere-schule/schulkonzept/leitbild/)).

### Which Bildungsplan governs which grade

Because the Staudinger is a Schule besonderer Art and not a Gemeinschaftsschule, it is not formally bound by the Gemeinschaftsschulverordnung; but the city brochure, the school's own grade-9 split and the Kultusministerium's statement that Gemeinschaftsschulen, Realschulen and Werkrealschulen share the Gemeinsamer Bildungsplan Sekundarstufe I together imply that grades 7 and 10 are taught to the Bildungsplan 2016 Sek I on the three Niveaus, and grade 11 to the Oberstufe plan for Gemeinschaftsschulen. This is an inference in the notes and should be confirmed with the school. The Kultusministerium defines the three "abschlussbezogene Niveaus": G-Niveau (grundlegend) for the Hauptschulabschluss, M-Niveau (mittler) for the Realschulabschluss and E-Niveau (erweitert) for gymnasiales Niveau, differing in "Durchdringungstiefe, den jeweiligen Komplexitätsgrad oder den Umfang"; the Kerncurriculum covers 27 of 36 school weeks and the rest is Schulcurriculum ([KM, Rahmensetzungen](https://km.baden-wuerttemberg.de/de/schule/allgemeine-informationen-1/bildungsplaene/rahmensetzungen-/-neuerungen)).

### Grade 7: differentiated classes, no central exam, Informatik begins

Grade 7 sits inside Standardstufe 7/8/9, so the plan does not say which Teilkompetenzen are taught in which of the three years; the Jahrgangsteam's Arbeitspläne do. Students face no central exam. Assessment is through Klassenarbeiten written by the Jahrgangsteam and two Projektwochen per year (sample grade-7 themes: Mittelalter, English week, Tierhaltung, Klimaschutz, Theater), assessed on product and process ([Staudinger, Projektunterricht](https://staudinger-gesamtschule.de/eine-besondere-schule/schulkonzept/projektunterricht/)). All three Niveaus sit in the same classroom, which is exactly the situation that niveaudifferenzierte Aufgaben and the daily individualised-learning hour are meant to serve. The Ständige Wissenschaftliche Kommission (SWK) recommended guided exploration of LLMs in the early Sekundarstufe I and regular use only from grade 8, a position the KMK called "erörterungsbedürftig" rather than adopting ([KMK Handlungsempfehlung](https://www.kmk.org/fileadmin/Dateien/veroeffentlichungen_beschluesse/2024/2024_10_10-Handlungsempfehlung-KI.pdf); the SWK figure rests on a secondary summary, [FelloFish](https://www.fellofish.com/blog/impulspapier-der-swk)). For grade 7 this argues for teacher-mediated, in-lesson use rather than an open chat.

## Rules of the game: KMK, Baden-Württemberg, data protection, EU AI Act, procurement

### KMK: permissive on student use, hard line on grading

The Bildungsministerkonferenz (KMK) adopted its "Handlungsempfehlung für die Bildungsverwaltung zum Umgang mit Künstlicher Intelligenz in schulischen Bildungsprozessen" on 10 October 2024 ([KMK press release](https://www.kmk.org/aktuelles/pressearchiv/mitteilung/bildungsministerkonferenz-verabschiedet-handlungsempfehlung-zum-umgang-mit-kuenstlicher-intelligenz-1.html)). It is permissive toward student use: it foresees students being supported "durch KI-Anwendungen, bspw. als persönlichen Lerntutor", rejects a general ban on AI for homework as "weder zielführend und wünschenswert, noch durchhaltbar", and declines to fix grade thresholds ([KMK Handlungsempfehlung](https://www.kmk.org/fileadmin/Dateien/veroeffentlichungen_beschluesse/2024/2024_10_10-Handlungsempfehlung-KI.pdf)). It draws two lines that bind product design. First, "Leistungsbewertung ist und bleibt eine pädagogische und hoheitliche Aufgabe, die als Verwaltungsakt ... ausschließlich von Lehrkräften erfüllt werden kann", with Art. 22 DSGVO requiring "immer eine menschliche Letztentscheidung". Second, the Länder commit to "eine ländergemeinsame Schnittstellenlösung zum datenschutzkonformen und kostenfreien Zugang zu LLM im schulischen Bereich" so that schools are "von größeren Prüfaufwänden befreit"; that commitment became telli, now AIS.chat ([KMK Handlungsempfehlung](https://www.kmk.org/fileadmin/Dateien/veroeffentlichungen_beschluesse/2024/2024_10_10-Handlungsempfehlung-KI.pdf)). The KMK also calls for new exam formats ("hilfsmittelunterstützte, längerfristig vorbereitete, kollaborative sowie dialogische") and for a cross-Länder recommendation on the didactic quality of AI applications, whose status the notes could not locate. The BMBF-commissioned review of March 2025 adds that AI tools are most effective in secondary school, that generative AI is less suitable for young learners than specialised educational AI, and names "datenschutzkonforme APIs" such as fobizz or schulKI that need no individual student login as a compliant route ([BMBF review](https://www.empirische-bildungsforschung-bmbfsfj.de/img/KI_Review_20250318_Veroeffentlichung.pdf)).

### Baden-Württemberg: the only Land with AI learning in statute

Baden-Württemberg is described in a September 2026 Länder overview as the only Land with statutory regulation of school AI ([paukki.de](https://www.paukki.de/ki-regeln-bundeslaender/)). § 115b SchG, last amended 16 December 2025, states in Abs. 1 that digital teaching forms are "üblicher Bestandteil der pädagogischen Arbeit" and in Abs. 9 that "Das Anwenden automatisierter, anpassungsfähiger Verfahren ist zum Zweck der technischen Unterstützung und Förderung des individuellen Lernweges zulässig" ([§ 115b SchG](https://lxgesetze.de/schg/115b)). The conditions sit in the Digitalunterrichtsverordnung (DUVO) of 8 October 2024, in force 10 October 2024 ([GBl. 2024 Nr. 81](https://www.baden-wuerttemberg.de/de/service/gesetze-und-verordnungen/gesetzblatt/detail/2024-81); [Landtag Drs. 17/8235](https://www.landtag-bw.de/resource/blob/556698/97e41b9307b339a2c21888c4db3937ba/17_8235_D.pdf)). The DUVO's full text could not be fetched; the KM summary says schools may use external IT systems, advertising is prohibited, processing records and Art. 28 contracts are required, and the Schulleitung decides with the Schulaufsicht merely notified ([KM, Digitaler Unterricht](https://km.baden-wuerttemberg.de/de/schule/digitalisierung/digitaler-unterricht)). An adaptive tutor thus has a Landesrecht legal basis in BW that most Länder lack, but the exact DUVO conditions on "anpassungsfähige Verfahren" must be read before the design is final.

The Kultusministerium's position is explicit: AI is permitted "zum Einsatz der Förderung und Unterstützung des individuellen Lernwegs", but "KI-Anwendungen dürfen keine Noten vergeben oder andere wesentlichen schulischen Entscheidungen treffen", only DSGVO-compliant applications may be used, and Art. 9 DSGVO data may not be processed by AI systems ([KM, KI im Unterricht](https://km.baden-wuerttemberg.de/de/schule/digitalisierung/kuenstliche-intelligenz-im-unterricht)). The ZSL FAQ adds that students "müssen darüber informiert werden, wenn KI-gestützte Systeme für Feedback eingesetzt werden" (§ 6 Abs. 3 DUVO), that AI may act as Korrekturhilfe but "Die endgültige Bewertung trifft jedoch immer die Lehrkraft", that AI detectors are unreliable, that AI proctoring is banned under KI-VO Art. 5(f), that student texts with Schöpfungshöhe need a Nutzungsrechteeinräumung before AI correction, and that KI systems evaluating learning outcomes are Hochrisiko-Systeme with obligations it still dates to August 2026 ([ZSL KI-FAQ](https://zsl-bw.de/,Lde/Startseite/lernen-ueberall/ki-faq)).

The Personalvertretung has co-determination rights when automated processing systems are introduced ([VwV Datenschutz 2019](https://it.kultus-bw.de/site/pbs-bw-km-root/get/documents_E1427590149/KULTUS.Dachmandant/KULTUS/Dienststellen/it.kultus-bw/Datenschutz%20an%20Schulen%20nach%20neuer%20EU%20DSGVO/dl-rechtsgrundlagen/VwV-Datenschutz-an-oeffentlichen-Schulen%20.pdf)), the Schulkonferenz decides school rules, and the ministry itself "observes the market" and declines to evaluate commercial tools ([Landtag Drs. 17/6910](https://www.landtag-bw.de/resource/blob/266862/b66fa82eaa95ba607f54d4b77a6616d3/17_6910_D.pdf)): there is no BW whitelist, so approval runs school by school.

Devices are now regulated too. The rules target "privat erworbene und nicht für den Unterrichtsgebrauch mitgeführte Geräte", so a school may write lesson use of the app on personal phones into its Schulordnung; the safer bet remains school iPads in class and phones at home.

### Data protection: legal basis is school law, not consent

In a BW public school the legal basis for processing student data is Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. e DSGVO with § 4 LDSG BW and § 115b SchG; the LfDI BW's guidance "Rechtsgrundlagen im Datenschutz beim Einsatz von Künstlicher Intelligenz" v2.0 (17 October 2024) says public bodies rely on this basis, that consent is difficult because "the data subject must understand the functionality of the data processing", and that an Auftragsverarbeitung is exceeded "when input data also benefits system improvement" for the provider ([LfDI BW, Rechtsgrundlagen KI](https://www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Rechtsgrundlagen-KI-v2.0.pdf)). School-provided accounts for educational purposes need no parental consent ([KM FAQ Datenschutz](https://it.kultus-bw.de/site/pbs-bw-rebrush2024/get/documents_E1205840066/KULTUS.Dachmandant/KULTUS/Dienststellen/it.kultus-bw/Datenschutz%20an%20Schulen%20nach%20neuer%20EU%20DSGVO/dl-service/FAQ%20Datenschutz%20an%20Schulen%20EUDSGVO.pdf)), and VIDIS states that consent would not be voluntary given compulsory attendance ([VIDIS Datenschutz](https://www.vidis.schule/datenschutz-bei-vidis/)). For a direct-to-consumer channel the picture flips: Art. 8 DSGVO requires parental consent below 16 for information society services ([Art. 8 DSGVO](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-8-gdpr/)), and BW practice is parents decide under 14, student plus parents from 14, student alone from 16 ([KM FAQ Datenschutz](https://it.kultus-bw.de/site/pbs-bw-rebrush2024/get/documents_E1205840066/KULTUS.Dachmandant/KULTUS/Dienststellen/it.kultus-bw/Datenschutz%20an%20Schulen%20nach%20neuer%20EU%20DSGVO/dl-service/FAQ%20Datenschutz%20an%20Schulen%20EUDSGVO.pdf)); the notes found no primary source confirming that Germany made no use of the Art. 8 opening clause, although this is widely reported.

A DSFA is effectively mandatory. The LfDI BW's Muss-Liste names "Einsatz von künstlicher Intelligenz zur Verarbeitung personenbezogener Daten zur Steuerung der Interaktion mit den Betroffenen oder zur Bewertung persönlicher Aspekte der Betroffenen" as always requiring a DSFA ([LfDI Muss-Liste](https://www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/Liste-von-Verarbeitungsvorg%C3%A4ngen-nach-Art.-35-Abs.-4-DS-GVO-LfDI-BW.pdf)), the DSK's Orientierungshilfe says a DSFA "is typically required for LLM deployments", prefers closed systems, requires an Art. 28 contract with the LLM provider and privacy by default with training and history retention disabled ([DSK OH KI](https://www.datenschutzkonferenz-online.de/media/oh/20240506_DSK_Orientierungshilfe_KI_und_Datenschutz.pdf)), and the VwV Datenschutz requires a DSFA when minors' data are processed automatically ([VwV Datenschutz](https://it.kultus-bw.de/site/pbs-bw-km-root/get/documents_E1427590149/KULTUS.Dachmandant/KULTUS/Dienststellen/it.kultus-bw/Datenschutz%20an%20Schulen%20nach%20neuer%20EU%20DSGVO/dl-rechtsgrundlagen/VwV-Datenschutz-an-oeffentlichen-Schulen%20.pdf)). The product should ship a ready-made DSFA the school can adopt.

US providers are the sore point. The LfDI BW banned Microsoft 365 from schools by summer 2022 after a pilot could not stop "zahlreiche Datentransfers in die USA" ([LfDI MS 365](https://www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de/ms-365-schulen-hinweise-weiteres-vorgehen/)). The EU-US Data Privacy Framework survived at first instance on 3 September 2025 ([Streamlex](https://streamlex.eu/news/general-court-ruling-latombe-commission/)) but is on appeal as C-703/25 P and the LfDI notes the contested PCLOB independence ([LfDI TB 2025](https://www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/LfDI-TB25-DATENSCHUTZ.pdf)). What passes in practice is a proxy architecture: fobizz's Hessen AVV lists OpenAI Ireland, Microsoft Corporation (USA), Anthropic Ireland, Mistral and Aleph Alpha as sub-processors under Standardvertragsklauseln while sending students only a pseudonymous identifier retained 24 hours or 12 months ([fobizz AVV Hessen](https://l.fobizz.com/pdfs/fobizz-avv-hessen-17-november-2024.pdf)); AIS.chat uses EU-only model hosting and pseudonymous VIDIS login ([AIS.chat FAQ](https://www.ais-chat.schule/faq)). Self-hosted open weights in the EU, as F13 does, remove the third-country question entirely.

### EU AI Act: limited-risk by design, high-risk if you grade or profile

Annex III point 3(b) of the AI Act classifies as high-risk "AI systems intended to be used to evaluate learning outcomes, including when those outcomes are used to steer the learning process of natural persons in educational and vocational training institutions at all levels" ([AI Act Annex III](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/annex/3/)). Article 6(3) exempts narrow procedural tasks, preparatory tasks and improvements of a completed human activity, but "an AI system referred to in Annex III shall always be considered to be high-risk where the AI system performs profiling of natural persons" ([AI Act Article 6](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/6/)). The Commission's draft classification guidelines of 19 May 2026 indicate, per a law-firm summary, that only "summative evaluations (i.e., those leading to a grade or qualification and affecting a student's educational trajectory)" are typically high-risk while "ongoing feedback" is generally not, that disclaimers in terms of service do not limit intended purpose, and that human review does not remove the classification ([Freshfields #32](https://www.freshfields.com/en/our-thinking/blogs/technology-quotient/eu-ai-act-unpacked-32-draft-commission-guidelines-on-high-risk-ai-implicati-102n1bn)); the draft itself could not be read, the consultation deadline is reported as 23 June 2026 by some firms and 23 July 2026 by others, and final adoption is expected "later in 2026 or early 2027" ([enz.ai](https://www.enz.ai/european-commission-draft-guidelines-high-risk-ai-classification)). The LfDI BW's 2024 report, via a secondary summary, treats adaptive systems steering the learning process as high-risk and advises schools to avoid AI for grading and exams ([regina-stoiber.com summary](https://regina-stoiber.com/2025/04/07/taetigkeitsbericht-lfdi-datenschutz/)).

Because public schools as deployers of a high-risk system would carry Art. 26 and 27 duties, a non-high-risk classification is also a sales argument. ## What the evidence says about AI in learning

### Pre-LLM tutoring systems: real but moderate gains, and no two sigma

The decades of intelligent tutoring system (ITS) research set the realistic benchmark. VanLehn's 2011 synthesis found human tutoring at d = 0.79, step-based ITS at 0.76, substep-based ITS at 0.40 and answer-based computer-aided instruction at 0.31, with effectiveness plateauing once feedback reaches the level of individual reasoning steps ([VanLehn 2011](https://www.emergingedtech.com/-kvanlehn/Stringent/PDF/EffectivenessOfTutoring_Vanlehn.pdf)). Kulik and Fletcher's 50-study review reported a median 0.66 SD but 0.73 on locally designed tests versus 0.13 on standardised measures ([ERIC EJ1090502](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1090502); breakdown via [du Boulay](http://users.sussex.ac.uk/~bend/papers/meta-reviews.pdf)). The newest K-12-specific meta-analysis (18 US studies, 77 effects) gives g = 0.271 overall, 0.253 for maths, 0.449 for interventions of 2 to 3 months and 0.110 for those over 6 months, with "provision of worked-out examples" the top moderator ([Leite et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.04997)). Large product RCTs land lower still: Cognitive Tutor Algebra I showed no effect in year one and about 0.20 SD in year two across 147 school sites ([RAND RB-9746](https://www.rand.org/content/dam/rand/pubs/research_briefs/RB9700/RB9746/RAND_RB9746.pdf)), ASSISTments g = 0.18 in Maine after one year of grade-7 homework use ([SRI](https://www.sri.com/publication/education-learning-pubs/digital-learning-pubs/how-big-is-that-reporting-the-effect-size-and-cost-of-assistments-in-the-maine-homework-efficacy-study/)) and g = 0.10 a year after the intervention in the North Carolina replication ([WestEd](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED630781.pdf)). Bloom's two-sigma claim rested on two unpublished dissertations with effects of 0.84 SD on narrow tests but 0.27 on broad ones ([Education Next](https://www.educationnext.org/two-sigma-tutoring-separating-science-fiction-from-science-fact/)), and human tutoring across 96 RCTs averages 0.37 SD, with once-weekly tutoring showing minimal effects ([Nickow et al., NBER w27476](https://www.nber.org/papers/w27476)). The product team should benchmark against 0.1 to 0.25 SD on independent tests after a full year, plan for a year-one null, and treat step-level feedback plus worked examples as the mechanism to build.

Three implications follow. First, every secondary trial with positive transfer effects had teacher orchestration (teacher-introduced topics and reflection in Nigeria; human review of AI messages in the UK); no stand-alone consumer tutor has comparable positive secondary evidence yet, and no completed RCT of a stand-alone LLM tutor exists in European secondary schools. Second, supplying verified solutions to the model is what closes the accuracy gap: GPT-3.5 explanations had 57 to 90 percent error rates, GPT-4 6 percent ([AIED 2024](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-64312-5_29)), ChatGPT hints failed a quality check 32 percent of the time before mitigation ([PLOS ONE 2024](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0304013)), and the guarded tutors in Bastani and Kestin both received solutions rather than generating them. Third, activation beats conversational quality: Khan Academy redesigned Khanmigo in 2026 to activate automatically during practice after the Tennessee trial ([Khan Academy blog, August 2026](https://blog.khanacademy.org/what-i-found-compelling-in-a-new-randomized-trial-of-khan-academy-in-math-intervention/)), and its own A/B tests over 15 million threads found that giving the tutor the student's recent problem history improved next-item correctness by 3.4 percent ([Khan Academy blog, May 2026](https://blog.khanacademy.org/how-khan-academy-is-building-a-better-ai-tutor-our-most-recent-learnings/)).

### Harm evidence: performance up, learning flat

The strongest harm signal is a dissociation between assisted performance and unassisted learning. Beyond Bastani's minus 17 percent, Fan et al.'s randomised experiment with 117 university students found ChatGPT users improved essay scores significantly but gained no more knowledge or transfer than other groups, with process traces showing "repeated loops of consulting ChatGPT", which the authors call "metacognitive laziness" ([BJET](https://ttim.phbern.ch/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Brit-J-Educational-Tech-2024-Fan-Beware-of-metacognitive-laziness-Effects-of-generative-artificial-intelligence-on.pdf)); a Corvinus preprint found an AI-permitted arm outscoring on midterms yet scoring at chance on an offline paper test ([arXiv 2510.16019](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.16019v1)). Stanford SCALE's 2026 review summarises that performance gains often disappear when students are assessed without AI, while tutoring-specific chatbots with step-by-step reasoning performed equivalently to textbooks and general-purpose tools underperformed ([SCALE report](https://scale.stanford.edu/sites/default/files/The%20Evidence%20Base%20on%20AI%20in%20K-12%20Report.pdf)). The widely cited MIT "Your Brain on ChatGPT" EEG study (54 participants, preprint) and Gerlich's cross-sectional survey are weak evidence: a commentary notes the MIT study would need about N = 159 for adequate power and that the search-engine group showed no impairment ([arXiv 2601.00856](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.00856)), and Gerlich is self-report with a published correction ([MDPI notes](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4698/15/1/6/notes)). Cite them as hypotheses.

Dishonesty did not spike: Stanford surveys in three high schools found monthly self-reported dishonest behaviour at 60 to 83 percent before ChatGPT and 60 to 64 percent in 2023 ([Computers and Education: AI](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666920X24000560)), but 63 percent of US teachers reported students disciplined for suspected AI use in 2023/24 and 50 percent said AI made them more distrustful ([Education Week](https://www.edweek.org/technology/new-data-reveal-how-many-students-are-using-ai-to-cheat/2024/04)). Anthropic's own analysis of 574,740 higher-education conversations found 47 percent "Direct" answer-seeking and Claude handling Creating (39.8 percent) and Analyzing (30.2 percent) most often ([Anthropic Education Report](https://anthropic.com/news/anthropic-education-report-how-university-students-use-claude)). Equity effects depend on design: ASSISTments helped low prior achievers ([SRI](https://www.sri.com/publication/education-learning-pubs/digital-learning-pubs/how-big-is-that-reporting-the-effect-size-and-cost-of-assistments-in-the-maine-homework-efficacy-study/)), Steenbergen-Hu and Cooper found low achievers "seemed to do worse" with K-12 maths ITS ([du Boulay](http://users.sussex.ac.uk/~bend/papers/meta-reviews.pdf)), and Nigeria's largest gains went to girls and to students with higher initial performance ([World Bank](https://ideas.repec.org/p/wbk/wbrwps/11125.html)). Tools embedded in teacher practice help weaker students; stand-alone drill and unguarded chat do not.

### Learning science that should drive the design

The robust, replicated principles are well known and point the same way as the trials. Practice testing and distributed practice have high utility across ages, interleaving and self-explanation moderate utility, rereading and highlighting low utility ([Dunlosky, AFT summary](https://www.aft.org/ae/fall2013/dunlosky)); conditions that make performance improve rapidly "often fail to support long-term retention and transfer" ([Bjork and Bjork 2011](https://bjorklab.psych.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/2016/07/EBjork_RBjork_2011.pdf)); worked examples beat problem solving for novices and should fade into completion problems as expertise grows ([Sweller et al. 2019](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-019-09465-5)); high-information feedback reaches about d = 0.99 while reinforcement scores 0.24 and praise 0.14 ([Wisniewski et al. 2020](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.03087/full); [Hattie and Timperley](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3102/003465430298487)); formative assessment yields 0.4 to 0.7 and "helps low achievers more than other students" while marks alone fail to improve learning ([Black and Wiliam](https://kappanonline.org/inside-the-black-box-raising-standards-through-classroom-assessment/)); and autonomy-supportive design beats controlling rewards ([Ryan and Deci 2020](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0361476X20300254)). The Rohrer grade-7 interleaving RCT is widely reported at d = 0.83 but could not be verified from primary sources ([APA record](https://psycnet.apa.org/record/2019-50616-001)).

### What this means for the product: twelve design rules

The evidence converges on a short rule set, each rule traceable to the studies above: (1) ground the tutor in verified content, supplying worked solutions, rubrics and teacher-noted misconceptions (Bastani, Kestin, Eedi, Khan's maths verifier); (2) withhold final answers and give step-level hints (VanLehn, Bastani, Tutor CoPilot); (3) require student production before feedback and give information-rich process feedback rather than praise or marks (Wisniewski, Black and Wiliam, Fan); (4) start novices with worked examples and fade (Sweller, Leite); (5) schedule spacing, interleaving and delayed low-stakes checks and make unassisted delayed performance the KPI (Dunlosky, Bjork, Bastani); (6) make the tutor proactive and embedded because students will not initiate help (Oreopoulos and Low, Khan redesign); (7) keep a human in the loop through teacher assignment, review and dashboards (Nigeria, Eedi, ASSISTments); (8) calibrate trust by showing uncertainty and inviting verification ([Lee et al., Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/lee_2025_ai_critical_thinking_survey.pdf)); (9) treat prompt-only guardrails as insufficient, since RL-trained pedagogy outperforms "do not give the answer" prompting ([Google RL paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.15607v1)) and OpenAI itself admits "inconsistent behavior" in study mode ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-study-mode/)), so run adversarial answer-leakage tests in QA; (10) support autonomy and competence rather than streaks and leaderboards; (11) separate assessment of learning from in-app success and make logs transparent to teachers; (12) plan for implementation maturity with year-one nulls (RAND, Oreopoulos and Low). All four major tutor products (Khanmigo, LearnLM, OpenAI study mode, Anthropic Learning mode) converge on "guide, do not give the answer", but LearnLM's 31 percent expert preference over GPT-4o is a behaviour rating, not a learning outcome ([arXiv 2412.16429](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.16429v1)), and Anthropic's Learning mode launched without an evaluation framework ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/introducing-claude-for-education)). A school product should run its own outcome study.

### Teacher-facing evidence: modest, real, and the only RCT on teacher time

The only RCT on teacher time is the EEF trial evaluated by NFER (259 teachers, 68 English secondary schools): ChatGPT with a guide cut Key Stage 3 science planning time from 81.5 to 56.2 minutes per week (31 percent) with "no noticeable difference in quality" on a limited sample ([EEF](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/news/teachers-using-chatgpt-alongside-a-guide-to-support-them-to-use-it-effectively-can-cut-lesson-planning-time-by-over-30-per-cent)). Survey evidence is larger but self-reported: 5.9 hours per week saved by weekly users among 2,232 US teachers ([Gallup and Walton](https://nextgeninsights.waltonfamilyfoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Teachers_AI_Report.pdf)) and 4 hours per week in Oak National Academy's impact report ([Oak](https://www.thenational.academy/blog/five-key-insights-from-our-2425-impact-report)). Teachers trust automated grading least: Anthropic's educator report found grading 48.9 percent automated yet rated the task where AI is least effective ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-education-report-how-educators-use-claude)), and in Germany the GEW cites research in which fobizz "generated entirely different grades" for identical work, "basically like rolling dice" ([GEW, February 2026](https://www.gew.de/aktuelles/detailseite/ki-erobert-die-schulen)). Every major review (Stanford SCALE with 20 high-quality causal studies out of 818 papers and zero in US K-12 classrooms, UNESCO's 2023 guidance proposing an age limit for independent GenAI conversations, OECD 2023) says adoption far outpaces evidence and policy should centre teachers, age-appropriate access and independent evaluation ([SCALE](https://scale.stanford.edu/sites/default/files/The%20Evidence%20Base%20on%20AI%20in%20K-12%20Report.pdf); [UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/guidance-generative-ai-education-and-research); [OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-digital-education-outlook-2023_c74f03de-en.html)). German evidence on telli or AIS.chat outcomes could not be retrieved; none appears to exist.

### Germany: from state pilots to a shared chatbot

Germany has consolidated on AIS.chat. Developed since 2024 by the FWU, the Länder's media institute, financed through the DigitalPakt and launched first in Bremen on 25 June 2025, telli was renamed AIS.chat on 20 May 2026 when the trademark licence expired on 19 May 2026 ([FWU press release](https://fwu.de/presse/neuer-name-fuer-den-ki-chatbot/)). As of September 2026 it is active in 12 Länder (Baden-Württemberg, Bayern, Berlin, Brandenburg, Bremen, Hessen, Niedersachsen, NRW, Saarland, Sachsen-Anhalt, Schleswig-Holstein, Thüringen) and not yet in Hamburg, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Rheinland-Pfalz and Sachsen ([AIS.chat FAQ](https://www.ais-chat.schule/faq); [ki-durchblick.com](https://ki-durchblick.com/ais-chat-telli-kostenlose-ki-deutsche-schulen-2026/)); the Deutsches Schulportal lists Hamburg and Sachsen too, so their status is uncertain ([Deutsches Schulportal](https://deutsches-schulportal.de/unterricht/fobizz-schulki-und-co-welche-ki-tools-koennen-schulen-nutzen/)). Models offered, all "operated in the EU", include OpenAI GPT-5.5, GPT-5-nano and GPT-5.6-luna, Meta Llama 3.3 70B and 3.1 8B, Mistral Nemo, Google Gemini 3.1 Lite and Anthropic Claude Sonnet 4.6 (added early August 2026) ([AIS.chat FAQ](https://www.ais-chat.schule/faq); [FWU, Sachsen-Anhalt launch](https://fwu.de/presse/ais-chat-startet-in-sachsen-anhalt/)); students join time-limited, content-predefined Lernszenarien by QR code or link and need no account; each Kultusministerium sets a monthly token budget (example about EUR 6 per month) ([ki-durchblick.com](https://ki-durchblick.com/ais-chat-telli-kostenlose-ki-deutsche-schulen-2026/)). The EdTech-Verband estimates the AIS project at EUR 50 to 60 million and criticised it on 1 April 2026 for crowding out private vendors ([news4teachers](https://www.news4teachers.de/2026/05/staatliche-unterrichts-ki-aus-telli-wird-ais-chat-und-schulen-muessen-schon-wieder-alles-aendern/); [EdTech-Verband](https://www.edtech-verband.de/)). No usage figures for AIS.chat were published in any source fetched.

The German classroom in 2026/27 looks like this. Teachers: weekly-or-more AI use rose to 25 percent (from 11 percent in 2025) among 1,547 teachers surveyed in November and December 2025, but only 29 percent of AI-using teachers let students use AI in lessons and just 14 percent use it for individualised learning offers; 67 percent expect negative effects on critical thinking, 52 percent want training, 48 percent feel secure ([Deutsches Schulbarometer 2026](https://bosch-stiftung.de/sites/default/files/publications/pdf/2026-06/RBSG_Deutsches_Schulbarometer_Lehrkr%C3%A4fte_2026.pdf)). Students: 74 percent of 12 to 19-year-olds use AI for homework or learning (65 percent in 2024), 57 percent trust AI information, 68 percent struggle to regulate screen time ([JIM 2025](https://mpfs.de/app/uploads/2025/11/PM_JIM-2025.pdf)); 42 percent use ChatGPT for school, 16 percent ANTON, 43 percent fear false cheating accusations and 48 percent worry about inequality ([Vodafone Stiftung and Ipsos](https://www.vodafone-stiftung.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/KI_an_europaeischen_Schulen_Deutscher_Laenderbericht_IPSOS.pdf)). The Vodafone Stiftung's April 2026 study with the Hector Institute finds students mostly use AI "to compile information, solve tasks and create work results" and calls for "quality-assured, evidence-based AI systems" ([Vodafone Stiftung](https://www.vodafone-stiftung.de/ki-in-der-bildung-abhaengigkeit-zu-handlungsfaehigkeit/)). Devices: an expert commission recommended in June 2026 banning private devices through grade 7 and the Leopoldina through grade 10; 28 percent of secondary teachers say they cannot teach digitally without student phones and 74 percent say smartphones hinder concentration ([Deutsches Schulportal, July 2026](https://deutsches-schulportal.de/schulkultur/handyverbot-an-schulen-ja-oder-nein-was-sagen-die-studien/); [bitkom, September 2026](https://www.bitkom.org/Presse/Presseinformation/Streitpunkt-Smartphone-jede-dritte-Lehrkraft-ausreichend-unterstuetzt)). Infrastructure: "Municipal financial capacity increasingly determines digital education opportunities" ([Vodafone Stiftung, March 2026](https://www.vodafone-stiftung.de/strukturelle-defizite-bremsen-digitale-bildung-in-deutschland/)).

The gap between student use (74 percent at home) and teacher-led use (29 percent in class) is where a German product's value lies: structured, teacher-visible practice and feedback, not another chatbot.

### Transferable lessons for a grade 7 to 11 app in Baden-Württemberg

Six lessons recur across every system. Train teachers before students get tools: Korea's 98.5 percent "insufficient training" verdict preceded collapse, Estonia's five months of teacher Professional Learning Communities preceded 65 percent weekly teacher activity. Design for school-managed tablets and lesson time with teacher-controlled activation and pseudonymous login, because phone rules now bind in BW and across Europe. Adopt England's product-safety norms (no final answers by default, session caps, no sycophancy, no engagement-maximising design) since they align with German pedagogy and with the AI Act's grading prohibition. Treat 30 to 50 percent weekly student engagement as the realistic target and build teacher assignment flows rather than relying on voluntary use. Expect to compete with or complement free AIS.chat and fobizz, so differentiate on curriculum-aligned structure and feedback. Budget on the order of EUR 100 to 200 per student per year as the upper reference (Estonia), with free state tools setting the floor at zero. And because no system has produced robust evidence of learning gains from student-facing generative AI, the credible 2026 claims are about teacher time, formative feedback quality and structured practice.

### International benchmarks and business models

Three models dominate. District or school licences at roughly USD 10 to 35 per student per year: Khanmigo was cut from USD 60 to 35 in November 2023 ([THE Journal](https://thejournal.com/articles/2023/11/16/khan-academy-cuts-district-price-of-khanmigo-ai-teaching-assistant.aspx)) and is now USD 15 per student per year with a 250-student minimum, with a USD 10 base for Khan Academy Districts ([Khan Academy blog](https://blog.khanacademy.org/becoming-a-khan-academy-districts-partner/)). Flat per-school subscriptions: Sparx Maths "from GBP 600 per year" scaling by school size ([EdTech Impact](https://edtechimpact.com/products/sparx-maths-homework/)), Third Space Learning GBP 5,000 per year for all secondary schools for unlimited sessions with its voice AI tutor "Skye" ([Third Space Learning](https://thirdspacelearning.com/pricing/)). Cheap B2C: Khanmigo at USD 4 per month or 44 per year, US only, under-18s via parent accounts ([khanmigo.ai/learners](https://www.khanmigo.ai/learners)). The strongest independent evidence sits with UK maths products: Sparx usage of one hour per week was associated with about 30 percent of a GCSE grade per year, with "no evidence that access to Sparx Maths alone" helps ([Cambridge key findings](https://www.educ.cam.ac.uk/research/programmes/sparx/SparxKeyFindings.pdf)); Eedi reports an RCT effect of d = 0.46 at 18 months and released its Misconception Graph of 8,000+ maths misconceptions under CC BY 4.0 ([eedi.com](https://eedi.com/)). Khan Academy content is CC BY-NC-SA and cannot be used in a paid product ([Khan Academy support](https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/202262954-Can-I-use-Khan-Academy-s-content-in-my-own-project)).

### Market size and the German EdTech funding reality

Germany has about 11.5 million students in 2025/26, about 9 million at allgemeinbildende Schulen ([Destatis](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2026/03/PD26_081_211.html)). Baden-Württemberg has 1,046,700 students at public allgemeinbildende Schulen, of whom about 93,400 attend Gemeinschaftsschulen, 196,800 Realschulen, 39,000 Werkreal- and Hauptschulen and 260,000 Gymnasien ([Statistisches Landesamt BW, February 2026](https://www.statistik-bw.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/pressemitteilung/ueber-104-millionen-schuelerinnen-und-schueler-an-oeffentlichen-schulen-in-baden-wuerttemberg/)); Gemeinschaftsschule enrolment is projected to rise to 105,000 by 2032/33 ([Statistisches Monatsheft 1/2026](https://www.statistik-bw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Service/Veroeff/Statistisches_Monatsheft/Beitrag_26_01_02.pdf)). The addressable core for a Bildungsplan-Sek-I product on the M and G Niveaus is therefore about 330,000 non-Gymnasium Sek I students in BW; at EUR 10 per student per year that is a ceiling of about EUR 3.3 million per year in BW alone, which is why incumbents go for Land contracts or B2C. The 320 Gemeinschaftsschulen alone are about EUR 1 million per year.

German EdTech is structurally underfunded: only 4 percent of 493 EdTech startups are VC-financed (versus 19 percent of all startups), 65 percent name sales as the central challenge and 89 percent say institutional customers are reluctant to adopt innovation ([EdTech Startup Monitor 2024](https://foundersfoundation.de/content-library/edtech-startup-monitor-2024/)); sales cycles are multi-year with no standard procurement and no low-threshold certification ([News4teachers](https://www.news4teachers.de/2025/07/bildungsmarkt-warum-edtech-startups-es-in-deutschland-besonders-schwer-haben/)). 2025 rounds were small (Knowunity EUR 27M, Edurino EUR 17M, Yuno EUR 7M, ubiMaster EUR 7M, [deutsche-startups.de](https://www.deutsche-startups.de/2025/08/19/aufstrebende-edtech-startups/)). Public attitudes are cautious: 57 percent of Germans see AI in schools as a danger and 34 percent as a chance, while 77 percent want teachers to convey responsible AI use ([Vodafone Stiftung, Civey 2023](https://www.vodafone-stiftung.de/ki-an-schulen/)).

### The gap a newcomer can credibly fill

No product found combines (a) explicit mapping to the Bildungsplan 2016 with G/M/E Niveau differentiation, (b) a teacher-student feedback loop with persistent learning records, (c) EU or German sovereign hosting that satisfies a Schulträger, and (d) parent transparency. The free state tools stop at chat (AIS.chat students have no persistent accounts or records, [ki-durchblick.com](https://ki-durchblick.com/ais-chat-telli-kostenlose-ki-deutsche-schulen-2026/)); the B2C apps stop at the student (Knowunity has no teacher plan); the curriculum-mapped products with evidence (bettermarks, Sparx) are maths-only and pre-LLM. Five differentiation claims are each backed by a verified gap: Bildungsplan-mapped, Niveau-differentiated tutoring for Gemeinschaftsschulen and Realschulen; "Lernentwicklungsbericht-ready" dashboards that turn practice logs into evidence for the Coachinggespräche the GMS model prescribes ([KM, Gemeinschaftsschule](https://km.baden-wuerttemberg.de/de/schule/gemeinschaftsschule/allgemeine-informationen-zur-gemeinschaftsschule)); cheating-safe by design (Socratic mode, visible logs, no essay generation), addressing the 43 percent of students who fear false accusations; sovereign hosting with an open-source core, matching the F13, AIS.chat and schulKI norms; and Moodle or LTI integration so the school gets no new silo.

## Product concept: what to build

### Positioning

A curriculum-mapped practice-and-feedback tutor that teachers assign, embedded in the Arbeitsplan and the Jahrgangsteam's weekly rhythm, with the AI acting as a step-level coach inside structured practice rather than as an open chat. The product's one-line promise to a school is: every Teilkompetenz of the Bildungsplan, at the student's Niveau, practised with worked examples and instant step-level feedback, with the teacher seeing what each student mastered and where they struggle, in a form that feeds the Coachinggespräch and never produces a grade.

### The student loop

| Step | What happens | Why (evidence) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assignment | Teacher (or the Jahrgangsteam's Arbeitsplan) assigns a Teilkompetenz block at the student's Niveau G, M or E; grade 11 blocks are keyed to Operatoren and Klausur formats | Students do not self-initiate; structured practice drives the gains ([Oreopoulos and Low](https://edworkingpapers.com/sites/default/files/ai26-1551.pdf)) |
| Worked example | A fully worked example at that Niveau with self-explanation prompts | Worked examples are the top moderator in K-12 ITS ([Leite et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.04997)) and a core CLT effect ([Sweller et al.](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-019-09465-5)) |
| Faded practice | Completion problems, then independent items; interleaved with earlier Teilkompetenzen; spaced reviews scheduled | Guidance-fading, interleaving and spacing ([Dunlosky](https://www.aft.org/ae/fall2013/dunlosky); [Bjork](https://bjorklab.psych.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/2016/07/EBjork_RBjork_2011.pdf)) |
| Step-level coach | On an error or a stall, the tutor activates automatically with a hint ladder from a prompt to a worked step; the model receives the verified solution, the misconception map and the teacher's notes; final answers withheld by default; every maths claim verified by CAS | Step-level threshold ([VanLehn](https://www.emergingedtech.com/-kvanlehn/Stringent/PDF/EffectivenessOfTutoring_Vanlehn.pdf)); guarded tutor removes exam harm ([Bastani](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2422633122)); 51 percent correctness for unguarded chat |
| Production first | For Deutsch and Englisch writing tasks, the student must submit a draft before any feedback; feedback is process- and self-regulation-level and never a mark | High-information feedback d about 0.99, praise 0.14 ([Wisniewski](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.03087/full)); metacognitive laziness loops ([Fan](https://ttim.phbern.ch/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Brit-J-Educational-Tech-2024-Fan-Beware-of-metacognitive-laziness-Effects-of-generative-artificial-intelligence-on.pdf)) |
| Exam rehearsal (grade 10 and 11) | Timed Pflichtteil drills without calculator; Wahlteil-style modelling tasks; simulated 15-minute Kommunikationsprüfung with role cards and server-side speech; D1/D2 writing at target word counts | Formats from the [Ausführungsbestimmungen RSAP 2027](https://km.baden-wuerttemberg.de/fileadmin/redaktion/m-km/intern/PDF/Dateien/Realschule/Ausführungsbestimmungen_2027/Ausführungsbestimmungen_Realschulabschlussprüfung_2027.pdf) |
| Delayed check | Short unassisted retrieval checks days later; this, not in-app correctness, is the KPI shown to student and teacher | Performance-learning dissociation ([Bastani](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2422633122); [SCALE](https://scale.stanford.edu/sites/default/files/The%20Evidence%20Base%20on%20AI%20in%20K-12%20Report.pdf)) |
| Reflection | A Logbuch entry: what I practised, what I got wrong, what I will do next; optional AI prompt to articulate the misconception | Matches the school's Logbuch and Coaching routines ([Staudinger](https://staudinger-gesamtschule.de/eine-besondere-schule/schulkonzept/individuelles-lernen/)) |

Free-form chat exists only inside an assigned context (the current item, the current text), is visible to the teacher, and discloses at first interaction that the student is talking to an AI, as Art. 50 AI Act and § 6 Abs. 3 DUVO require. There are no streaks, leaderboards or push-driven engagement loops: the DfE's January 2026 standards expect no design "to prolong use, for increased engagement or revenue", hard session caps teachers can override, and no anthropomorphisation ([DfE product safety standards](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/generative-ai-product-safety-standards/generative-ai-product-safety-standards)), and the Commission's DSA guidelines recommend disabling streaks and autoplay for minors ([Winston Taylor](https://www.winstontaylor.com/insights/implementing-article-28-dsa-ec-guidelines-on-protection-of-minors-under-the-digital-services-act)).

### The teacher loop

Teachers get three things. An assignment builder that starts from the Bildungsplan tree and the Jahrgangsteam's Arbeitsplan and generates niveaudifferenzierte item sets they can edit (the only RCT-grade benefit of AI for teachers is planning time, [EEF](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/news/teachers-using-chatgpt-alongside-a-guide-to-support-them-to-use-it-effectively-can-cut-lesson-planning-time-by-over-30-per-cent)). A class view of mastery per Teilkompetenz and Niveau, with the misconceptions the tutor saw most often, framed as formative evidence; scores are proposals the teacher confirms, never marks, and nothing flows to a Zeugnis. And a Coaching-Protokoll draft for each student before the twice-yearly Coachinggespräch, summarising practised competences, delayed-check results and the student's own Logbuch reflections, which the teacher edits. Teacher review of AI feedback on writing before release (the Singapore and Eedi pattern) is the default for Deutsch and Englisch in year one.

Maths first is a deliberate narrowing. It is the subject where the product can be fully verifiable, where the exam is central and dated, where the school's own differentiation is most itemised, and where every evidence-backed product (Cognitive Tutor, ASSISTments, Sparx, Eedi, bettermarks) has started. Deutsch and Englisch feedback depend on rubric-based model judgement, which the German-language evidence base cannot yet validate (no published benchmark for German essay feedback quality was found), so they follow with teacher review.

### Parent-facing transparency

Parents get a plain German page on what is stored and for how long, a view of time used and topics practised (mirroring Apple's Screen Time model), a report button in every AI reply and no feature that requires the child's email. This matches Denmark's requirement that teachers inform parents about the school's AI approach ([Eurydice Denmark](https://eurydice.eacea.ec.europa.eu/national-education-systems/denmark/national-reforms-general-school-education)), the fAIrChat parent-letter precedent ([Elternschreiben Eppingen](https://hartmanni-gymnasium-eppingen.de/download/index/202425/008Elternschreiben_Fairchat.pdf)) and the Staudinger's existing parent channels (WebUntis parent self-registration, a smartphone guidance page, [Staudinger](https://staudinger-gesamtschule.de/)).

## Technical architecture: web app on iPhone, iPad and PC, and the LLM layer

Clients never talk to a model directly: every turn passes through the app and the gateway, which sends routine practice to EU-hosted open weights and escalates hard turns and the content pipeline to Claude through an EU endpoint.

### Why web-first is structurally right in Germany

Three facts make a progressive web app (PWA) the only sensible client decision. VIDIS, the Länder's identity broker, accepts providers in "WebApp format (native apps not currently supported)" ([VIDIS Teilnahmeprozess](https://www.vidis.schule/teilnahmeprozess/)). SCHULE@BW is a browser-based "Hub" with SSO to external applications ([KM, Nutzungsordnung DBP](https://km.baden-wuerttemberg.de/de/schule/digitalisierung/digitale-bildungsplattform/nutzungsordnung-fuer-die-digitale-bildungsplattform)), and the Staudinger's Moodle lives on that platform at 04158410170.moodle.bw.schule ([Staudinger, Service](https://staudinger-gesamtschule.de/service/)). And school iPads are managed devices on which teachers must approve every app ([Staudinger, Schulregeln](https://staudinger-gesamtschule.de/eine-besondere-schule/schulkonzept/schulregeln/)), whereas a web app can be pushed as an MDM Web Clip with "Full Screen" that opens "the URL as a web app without a browser" ([Apple, Web Clips payload](https://support.apple.com/guide/deployment/web-clips-payload-settings-depbc7c7808/web)).

### Identity, LMS integration and data minimisation

The lowest-friction identity path has two legs. LTI 1.3 Advantage launches from Moodle BW and itslearning deliver a stable pseudonymous user ID and role; Moodle 5.2 supports Deep Linking, Assignment and Grade Services and Names and Role Provisioning with admin toggles for sharing name and email ([MoodleDocs LTI](https://docs.moodle.org/502/en/Managing_LTI_External_tools)), and itslearning's default privacy level passes only "a unique ID for a user and their role" ([itslearning developer docs](https://developer.itslearning.com/LTI-configuration-information.html)). VIDIS OpenID Connect login transmits a per-provider pseudonym `sub`, `schulkennung`, `bundesland` and `rolle` (LEHR, LERN, LEIT), with names optional for teachers only, and requires a partner Land, a data-protection self-assessment reviewed by datenschutz nord, two-phase technical integration and per-Land approval ([VIDIS Whitepaper v60](https://www.vidis.schule/wp-content/uploads/sites/10/2024/08/Erweiterte-Inbetriebnahmephase-Whitepaper-zur-Anbindung-an-VIDIS-fuer-Service-Provider-v60-20240813_112511.pdf); [VIDIS Teilnahmeprozess](https://www.vidis.schule/teilnahmeprozess/)); 12 Länder were connected as of January 2026 with four more in process ([VIDIS Neuigkeiten](https://www.vidis.schule/neuigkeiten/)), and BW uses VIDIS for AIS.chat ([KM press release](https://km.baden-wuerttemberg.de/de/service/pressemitteilung/pid/ki-chatbot-fuer-schulen-geht-an-den-start)). For schools without VIDIS activation, a class-code join with teacher-created pseudonymous accounts (the ANTON pattern, [ANTON FAQ](https://anton.app/de/faq/)) is the German norm. The app should store only pseudonym, role, school ID and Land, let the school hold the mapping to names, never request birth dates, keep prompts and transcripts under a short retention (SCHULE@BW deletes logs after 90 days and identities 12 months after deactivation, [KM Datenschutzinformationen DBP](https://km.baden-wuerttemberg.de/de/schule/digitalisierung/digitale-bildungsplattform/datenschutzinformationen-der-digitalen-bildungsplattform)) and offer student- and teacher-initiated deletion. Prioritise LTI 1.3 and SchulConneX-compatible group claims over OneRoster, for which no German adoption is documented ([1EdTech OneRoster](https://www.1edtech.org/standards/oneroster); [SchulConneX](https://www.schulconnex.de/)).

### Client stack

Next.js with the Vercel AI SDK gives the most documented path for manifest, Web Push with VAPID, Serwist offline caching and streaming chat hooks ([Next.js PWA guide](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/guides/progressive-web-apps); [AI SDK](https://ai-sdk.dev/docs/introduction)); SvelteKit with vite-plugin-pwa yields smaller bundles for old iPads at the cost of less first-party PWA documentation ([Vite PWA](https://vite-pwa-org.netlify.app/guide/)). Either way: Workbox precaching plus runtime caching of lesson assets, chat streams that degrade to polling on Safari 18, KaTeX for maths rendering with MathJax 4 reserved for an accessibility "voice this formula" mode ([KaTeX](https://katex.org/); [MathJax](https://www.mathjax.org/)), and WCAG 2.2 AA as a procurement requirement because Schulträger are public bodies even where the startup is BFSG-exempt ([W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)). Shared iPads wipe all data on session exit ([Apple, Shared iPad](https://support.apple.com/guide/deployment/shared-ipad-overview-dep9a34c2ba2/web)), so per-device offline state cannot be relied on in school. Run from a small, stable set of domains so MDM admins can allowlist them in a "Specific Web Sites Only" Web Content Filter profile ([Apple, Web Content Filter](https://support.apple.com/guide/deployment/web-content-filter-payload-settings-depc77c9609/web)).

The unit economics support the school-licence shape, with one condition. Four hours of use per month on EU-hosted open weights cost an estimated EUR 0.05 to 0.35 per active student per month, which is EUR 0.60 to 4.20 per year, while the hybrid the architecture section assumes (80 percent of turns on gpt-oss class models, 20 percent escalated to Sonnet 5.5) costs about EUR 0.06 plus $0.08 per student-month. A EUR 2 to 5 per student per year licence therefore leaves margin for hosting, support and content only if the default turn runs at the cheap end of the open-weight range (gpt-oss-120b or Gemma 4 class), not on Qwen3.8-27B at Scaleway prices; the cost driver remains people (teacher onboarding, content curation, compliance), not tokens.

### Go-to-market sequence

The evidence supports a three-step stack: a teacher-champion pilot at one school on its existing Moodle; Schulträger purchases under DigitalPakt 2.0 or Bavaria-style per-student budgets; and a Land framework contract only after a multi-school evidence base. For the Staudinger specifically, the approach that BW administrators already know and accept is the fAIrChat pattern: teacher-enabled activation, no student accounts (LTI pseudonyms), no personal data in prompts, EU hosting, a parent information letter rather than a consent form, and an evaluation partner. The regulatory package to bring to the first meeting with the Schulleitung and the behördliche Datenschutzbeauftragte is: a draft AVV with TOMs and sub-processor list, a draft DSFA keyed to the LfDI Muss-Liste, an Art. 6(3) AI Act assessment and Art. 50 disclosure text, a Verzeichnis entry template, a Personalrat briefing and a one-page parent letter. The commercial argument to a Schulträger is not "AI" but "the Bildungsplan, differentiated, with evidence": instrument weekly active minutes per student from day one (the Sparx dosage logic), run Niveau-specific pre/post checks, and publish the pilot results; in the UK, evidence is what unlocked per-school subscriptions.

Three risks are specific to selling in Germany. Free state tools set the price floor at zero and are expanding (AIS.chat added image generation in February 2026 and Claude Sonnet 4.6 in August 2026). Procurement is multi-level and slow (Länder, Schulträger, Schulleitung, Lehrkräfte), with 65 percent of EdTechs naming distribution as the main problem ([News4teachers](https://www.news4teachers.de/2025/07/bildungsmarkt-warum-edtech-startups-es-in-deutschland-besonders-schwer-haben/)). And public sentiment is sceptical (57 percent see AI in schools as a danger), so the product's parent transparency and no-grading stance are sales features, not compliance overhead.

### Open questions the research could not close

School: per-grade numbers, Eingangsklasse size, iPad classes and MDM, the Stundentafel actually used, Leistungsfächer offered, any internal Medienkonzept or KI-Leitlinie, and Klassenarbeit formats under the Notenbildungsverordnung. Law: the full DUVO text and its conditions on "anpassungsfähige Verfahren"; the LfDI BW 2024 chapter on school AI in its original wording; whether any Land has approved a Claude- or Bedrock-based student-facing tool; the final Commission high-risk guidelines; BW's DigitalPakt 2.0 Förderrichtlinie and whether AI software is förderfähig; the Startchancen Anlage 3 permitted uses; eduCheck digital's status after March 2025. Curriculum: V3.0 Teilkompetenz tables for the sciences; the Fachplan for Informatik und Medienbildung; the Oberstufe GER targets for Englisch at GMS (404); BW's implementation of the KMK Oberstufenvereinbarung for Abitur 2029 and 2030. Technology: a German-specific LLM leaderboard snapshot; German WER comparisons for Whisper, Voxtral and Parakeet; German handwriting OCR benchmarks; Llama Guard 4 accuracy in German; whether MDM Full Screen web clips receive Web Push and the 7-day storage exemption like user-installed web apps; STACKIT's tier-to-model mapping; OVHcloud's AI Endpoints hosting region; the Llama 4 EU multimodal clause; tokens per tutoring hour (replace the assumption with telemetry). Market: Knowunity's and simpleclub's school offerings and paying-user counts; AIS.chat usage numbers; what Schulträger actually spend per school on software; a post-2015 Nachhilfe market size; parents' willingness to pay for learning apps.

The report closes on the one finding that outranks all others: no system anywhere has yet shown robust learning gains from student-facing generative AI, while the harm from unguarded chat is measured. The product that wins in Baden-Württemberg will be the one that treats the AI as a step-level coach inside practice that teachers assign, proves its dosage and delayed-retention numbers in public, and never asks a Schulleitung to defend a grade a machine produced.

### Grade 10: the Realschulabschlussprüfung 2027 and the Eingangsklasse threshold

### Grade 11: the Einführungsphase and the road to Abitur 2029

Grade 11 at a Gesamtschule is structurally a bridge year: the Einführungsphase mirrors Klasse 11 of the G9 Gymnasium, Versetzung into the Kursstufe is required, and grades from Klasse 11 are listed on the Abiturzeugnis but "haben jedoch keinen Einfluss auf die Gesamtqualifikation" ([Leitfaden Abitur 2027](https://km.baden-wuerttemberg.de/fileadmin/redaktion/m-km/intern/PDF/Publikationen/Gymnasium/2025_Leitfaden_fuer_die_gymnasiale_Oberstufe_Abitur_2027.pdf); [abschluss-bw.de, secondary](https://www.abschluss-bw.de/abitur/oberstufe)). The pedagogical priority is therefore readiness for Klausur formats, Operatoren (the standardised task verbs attached to each Oberstufen-Fachplan, [Englisch GMSO](https://www.bildungsplaene-bw.de/,Lde/LS/BP2016BW/ALLG/GMSO/E)) and the Leistungsfach choice. The GMS Klasse-11 Stundentafel gives Deutsch 4, Mathematik 4, Englisch 3, Französisch 3 (or a new language 4), Physik 2, Chemie 2, Biologie 1, Geschichte 2, a Profilfach 4 and a Pool of 2 ([KM, Bildungsplan GMS](https://km.baden-wuerttemberg.de/de/schule/gemeinschaftsschule/bildungsplan-2016-fuer-die-gemeinschaftsschule)). The Staudinger's Oberstufenberatung fixes three mandatory GFS (Gleichwertige Feststellung von Schülerleistungen, graded presentations or papers) across different subjects in the first three Halbjahre, an optional Seminarkurs that can replace an oral exam subject, and the Englisch Kommunikationsprüfung as tandem or individual ([Staudinger, Oberstufenberatung](https://staudinger-gesamtschule.de/schulgemeinschaft/studienstufe/oberstufenberatung/)).

The rules this cohort will face in the Kursstufe are the single biggest FLUX item in the notes. The Leitfaden for Abitur 2028 (published 16 December 2025) documents the current BW system: 3 Leistungsfächer at 5 Wochenstunden, at least 30 Basisfach plus 12 Leistungsfach courses, written exams in the 3 Leistungsfächer (modern languages with a 15-minute Kommunikationsprüfung) plus 2 oral exams, Block I at most 600 points and Block II at most 300, 900 total, 300 minimum and 823 or more for a 1.0, Klausuren twice per Halbjahr in Leistungsfächern, and the rule that GMS students who start a second foreign language in Klasse 11 must continue it for all four Halbjahre ([Leitfaden Abitur 2028](https://km.baden-wuerttemberg.de/fileadmin/redaktion/m-km/intern/PDF/Publikationen/Gymnasium/2025-12-16-Leitfaden-ABI-2028.pdf)). Wahlfächer that can become oral exam subjects include Informatik, Literatur und Theater, Psychologie, Philosophie, Wirtschaft, spät beginnendes Spanisch and the Vertiefungskurs Mathematik ([KM, FAQ zur neuen Oberstufe](https://km.baden-wuerttemberg.de/de/schule/gymnasium/abitur-und-oberstufe/faq-zur-neuen-oberstufe)).

Today's Eingangsklasse enters the Kursstufe in 2027/28 and sits the Abitur in 2029. The KMK Oberstufenvereinbarung (as amended 2023) requires the Länder to implement its provisions for cohorts "ab 2027 in die Einführungsphase", with 2 or 3 subjects at erhöhtes Anforderungsniveau, at least 40 Halbjahreskurse belegt and 36 eingebracht, Gesellschaftswissenschaften for at least 6 Halbjahre and Naturwissenschaften at 3 hours on grundlegendes Niveau ([KMK Vereinbarung](https://www.kmk.org/fileadmin/veroeffentlichungen_beschluesse/1972/1972_07_07-VB-gymnasiale-Oberstufe-Abiturpruefung.pdf)); secondary sources read the same agreement as applying to students entering the Qualifikationsphase in 2027 with Abitur 2030 at the latest ([Deutsches Schulportal](https://deutsches-schulportal.de/bildungswesen/neue-oberstufenvereinbarung-wie-das-abitur-in-zukunft-vergleichbarer-werden-soll/)), so the two readings conflict on whether this cohort is affected. Kultusministerin Schopper said in March 2023 that BW "requires minimal modifications" ([Staatsanzeiger, paywalled](https://www.staatsanzeiger.de/bildung/nachgehakt-baden-wuerttemberg-muss-laut-schopper-wenig-am-abitur-aendern/)), yet BW today counts all 40 courses in Block I rather than 36 and runs 2-hour science Basisfächer, and the KM's own Termine page states "No statements regarding curricular changes for 2029 or 2030" ([KM, Termine Abitur](https://km.baden-wuerttemberg.de/de/schule/gymnasium/termine-und-informationen-zum-abitur)); a Leitfaden for Abitur 2029 did not exist at the expected URL on 4 October 2026 ([404 check](https://km.baden-wuerttemberg.de/fileadmin/redaktion/m-km/intern/PDF/Publikationen/Gymnasium/Leitfaden-ABI-2029.pdf)). The product should assume Abitur-2028 rules as the baseline and keep every Kursstufe-dependent feature configurable. Grade 11 is also a small audience: the state counted 1,226 Sek-II students across all Gemeinschaftsschulen in 2024/25 (the Statistischer Bericht for the same year gives 1,520; [Statistisches Landesamt BW](https://www.statistik-bw.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/pressemitteilung/schuelerzahl-an-oeffentlichen-allgemeinbildenden-schulen-erneut-gestiegen/)), and the Staudinger's seven-form entry narrows to one Eingangsklasse cohort; the GEW's claim that "only 9 Gemeinschaftsschulen have upper levels" ([GEW BW](https://www.gew-bw.de/aktuelles/detailseite/g9-nicht-ohne-veraenderungen-in-der-sekundarstufe)) conflicts with the Kultusministerium's count of 14 Sek-II Gemeinschaftsschulen ([KM, Zahlen zur Schule](https://km.baden-wuerttemberg.de/de/schule/zahlen-zur-schule)).

### Content and licensing for the knowledge base

The Bildungsplan itself is a public official document with a PDF export and accompanying Beispielcurricula, Synopsen and Kompetenzraster per subject ([GMSO Mathematik](https://www.bildungsplaene-bw.de/,Lde/LS/BP2016BW/ALLG/GMSO/M)) and should be the schema of the knowledge base. Exercise content, however, cannot come from Schulbüchern. § 60a UrhG allows up to 15 percent of a published work for teaching but excludes works "ausschließlich für den Unterricht an Schulen geeignet, bestimmt und entsprechend gekennzeichnet" ([§ 60a UrhG](https://dejure.org/gesetze/UrhG/60a.html)); the Länder-publisher agreement caps copying at "15 %, maximal aber 20 Seiten" per Schuljahr and Klasse and states that "Inhalte aus Unterrichtswerken dürfen nicht in KI-Systeme und KI-Anwendungen geladen werden" ([schulbuchkopie.de](https://www.schulbuchkopie.de/)). That clause also means teacher uploads of textbook scans into the app would be a breach, and the product must say so in its terms.

### The LLM layer: gateway, routing and model mix

The runtime sits behind an OpenAI-compatible gateway. LiteLLM is MIT (except its enterprise folder) and provides routing, fallbacks, budgets, virtual keys and Langfuse logging ([LiteLLM](https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm)); OpenRouter adds EU-only processing on Business and Enterprise plans ([OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/docs/features/privacy-and-logging)). vLLM implements both the OpenAI API and the Anthropic Messages API, so prompts written against the Anthropic SDK during development port to local open weights unchanged ([vLLM docs](https://docs.vllm.ai/en/latest/)); Ollama exposes an OpenAI-compatible API but defaults to a 2,048-token context and auto-selects 4 or 1 parallel requests depending on memory, both of which must be raised for classroom use ([Ollama FAQ](https://github.com/ollama/ollama/blob/main/docs/faq.md)). Routing rule: the default tutoring turn goes to an EU-hosted or local open-weight 27B-class model; turns flagged as hard (multi-step proofs, essay feedback, ambiguous misconceptions) or failing a verifier escalate to Claude Sonnet 5.5 via an EU endpoint; the content pipeline (item generation, Bildungsplan tagging, misconception mapping, evaluation transcripts) runs on Claude through the Batch API at half price. Structured outputs are available on both sides (grammar-constrained on Claude, xgrammar or guidance on vLLM) and should force every maths step into a schema that SymPy verifies in a sandbox; gpt-oss itself moves from 92.5 to 97.9 percent on AIME 2025 with tools ([gpt-oss model card](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.10925)).

### Speech, handwriting, guardrails, observability and evaluation

German speech input is solved server-side: Whisper large-v3 (Apache 2.0), Whisper large-v3-turbo (MIT, 4 decoder layers, "minor quality degradation") and NVIDIA Parakeet-TDT-0.6B-v3 (CC BY 4.0, German FLEURS WER 5.04 percent) all run cheaply next to the LLM ([Whisper v3](https://huggingface.co/openai/whisper-large-v3); [Parakeet](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3)). German text-to-speech is the weak spot: Kokoro has no German, Piper (MIT, German voices) was archived in October 2025 with a GPL successor, and Orpheus's German fine-tune is gated ([Piper](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper); [Kokoro](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M)); use browser speechSynthesis or Mistral's hosted Voxtral TTS for a first release. For handwritten maths, TrOCR is effectively English-only ([TrOCR](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/trocr-large-handwritten)); Qwen3-VL-8B (OCR in 32 languages, robust to blur and tilt, [Qwen3-VL](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct)), Mathpix (from $0.002 per image, [Mathpix](https://mathpix.com/pricing)) or IONOS-hosted LightOnOCR 2 are the options, with no German school-handwriting benchmark published for any of them.

Guardrails for minors are layered: Llama Guard 4 12B (14 hazard categories incl. self-harm and sexual content, German supported, Llama 4 licence, [Llama Guard 4](https://huggingface.co/meta-llama/Llama-Guard-4-12B)) or NeMo Guardrails ([NVIDIA](https://docs.nvidia.com/nemo/guardrails/latest/index.html)) on input and output, Anthropic's Haiku 4.5 `is_harmful` screening pattern with untrusted content isolated in `tool_result` blocks ([Claude docs, mitigate jailbreaks](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/test-and-evaluate/strengthen-guardrails/mitigate-jailbreaks)), deterministic blocklists and teacher escalation; no German-language accuracy evaluation of Llama Guard 4 was found. Observability runs on Langfuse (Berlin GmbH, MIT core, EU cloud region or self-hosted, Core $29 per month, [Langfuse pricing](https://langfuse.com/pricing)). The tutoring evaluation must exist before model choice is final: a German adaptation of the LearnLM 29-question pedagogy rubric (gives\_away\_answers, challenges\_learner, keeps\_on\_topic, metacognition) scored by trained teachers on scenario transcripts ([LearnLM](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.16429)), plus automated checks for verbatim answer leakage before the student attempted, hallucinated Bildungsplan references and language level per grade, plus an adversarial "just give me the answer" test suite run on every prompt or model change. Fine-tuning is a later option: QLoRA on a 27B model needs about 22 GB VRAM ([Unsloth](https://unsloth.ai/docs/get-started/fine-tuning-for-beginners/unsloth-requirements.md)), and Google's RL-pedagogy work shows a 7B model can be trained to match LearnLM on pedagogy ([arXiv 2505.15607](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.15607v1)).
